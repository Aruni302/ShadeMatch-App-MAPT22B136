Fabric Photography Tips
1
Use D65 lighting. Shoot under standard daylight (D65 / 6500K) or near a north-facing window to minimise metamerism.
2
No flash. Camera flash creates harsh specular highlights that distort measured colour values.
3
Consistent distance. Maintain the same camera-to-fabric distance and angle across all batch photos.
4
Avoid shadows. Lay fabric flat on a neutral grey or white background, free of wrinkles and shadows.
5
Consistent conditions. Photograph all batches of the same order under identical lighting at the same time of day.
